# Authentication PRD: audit notes

Notes from auditing "Vardenia Authentication and Accounts" (PRD dated 20 November 2026), section by section, against the code on `feature/business-onboarding-form` and live tests on the development database.

Each note is one of:

- **Missing from the PRD**: true in the product, but the PRD does not say it. Add it to the next PRD version.
- **Correction**: the PRD says something the product does not do.
- **Fixed**: a gap found and fixed during the audit (the PRD can keep its wording).
- **Open**: needs a decision or a later step.

---

## Step 2. Who can do what

Verified live: sign-in for all three types; sessions of 7 days (customer), 4 hours (partner), 8 hours (team); lockout after 10 / 5 / 5 wrong passwords for 10 minutes; each account reads only its own record; a partner cannot add a listing to themselves or change their password with a session alone; staff cannot make themselves admin; cookies are HttpOnly and SameSite Lax.

| Type                 | Note                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fixed                | Partner invitations were never sent. Payload's anti-spam check for "forgot password" looks the account up outside the save that creates it, finds nobody and silently skips. Accounts created by staff or by publishing a submission had no link and no email. The invitation now creates its link inside the same save and sends the email itself.                                                                                                                                                |
| Fixed                | A partner's ordinary "forgot password" link lasted 3 days, because it was the same setting as the invitation. Now: invitation 3 days, ordinary reset 1 hour, and each email states its own lifetime.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Fixed                | "Forgot password" revealed that an account exists when the email could not be sent: the answer became an error showing the email provider's own message. It now always answers "Success" and records the failure on the server, for all three account types.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Correction           | Team password reset is not "through admin": the admin sign-in page has its own "Forgot password" email with a 1 hour link.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Correction           | Password reset row should read: customer 1 hour link; partner 1 hour link, except the first-time invitation (3 days); team 1 hour link.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Missing from the PRD | Sign-in is also limited to 10 requests per minute per internet connection, shared across all sign-in, forgot-password, reset, unlock and verify requests. It stops password guessing spread over many accounts, mass lockouts and reset-email spam. Known downside: people sharing one connection (mobile carriers, hotel or office Wi-Fi) share the budget and may see "Too many requests" for up to a minute. Suggested later: count per connection and email, with a higher cap per connection. |
| Missing from the PRD | "Forgot password" can be requested once per 15 seconds per account (Payload default).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Missing from the PRD | A failed sign-in counter resets on a successful sign-in; the lock lifts by itself after 10 minutes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

## Step 4. Customers

Verified live: sign-up gives the same "Check your email" answer for a new and an existing address; an unverified account cannot sign in ("Please verify your email"); verifying lets it sign in; signing up again with an existing address sends a reset email, and using it also verifies the account (how an older record is claimed); a used reset link cannot be used twice; 9 characters, 10 spaces and an empty name are refused at sign-up; pausing sign-ups from Settings works ("We are not opening new accounts right now"); a customer can rename themselves but cannot change their email or verification directly; closing needs the password, cancels upcoming bookings and keeps them as records.

| Type                 | Note                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fixed                | The 10-character rule lived only in the site's own forms: Payload's built-in reset address accepted the password "abc" (and partners and the team always reset there), and the admin, API and scripts allowed 3 characters. Now one rule (at least 10 characters, spaces alone do not count) is checked on the server for customers, partners and the team on every path: sign-up, both reset addresses, the profile page, and create or edit in the admin, API or scripts. Existing shorter passwords keep working until next changed. |
| Fixed                | The profile page accepted a password of 10 spaces, which sign-in then treats as empty, locking the customer out. It now applies the same rule.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Fixed                | The test database (also used by staging) was missing two booking safety triggers the live database has: the capacity check for places without room types and the upcoming bookings limit. Both reattached from the migrations' own SQL; the live database was never affected. Proven: the database itself refuses a 6th upcoming booking, and the 36-check Stay test still passes.                                                                                                                                                      |
| Correction           | Closing an account always anonymises it ("Closed account", random address), with or without bookings; it is never removed. Decided 20 November 2026: keep this (irreversible, so it counts as erasure, and it keeps reviews, saves and receipts consistent). Update the PRD wording.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Correction           | The sign-up rate limit is not its own "10 per minute per address": it is the shared sign-in budget of 10 per minute per internet connection (see step 2).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Missing from the PRD | Customer pages also include closing the account (`/account/close`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Missing from the PRD | Resetting from the email link verifies an unverified account, and expires the link.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

Same message, so low risk; noted.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

### Step 4, follow-up: what closing an account removes (decided 20 November 2026)

Goal: remove the customer and all their personal data, but keep every record, especially bookings.

| Type                 | Note                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fixed                | Closing an account now also deletes the customer's receipts (photo or PDF and the text read from it; the booking keeps the billed amount), deletes their reviews, erases name, email, phone and notes from their Event Staff requests (the request is kept as a record of the event), and deletes the questions they sent from the Questions page (matched on their email). Nothing is anonymised if any step fails. Verified live end to end. |
| Fixed                | Bookings are now kept for good as anonymous records (reference, place, dates, party size, status, rooms, amounts). The hourly job no longer deletes or cuts down bookings after 12 months; it still deletes scans after 12 months, error records after 90 days and receipts left without a booking after 12 months.                                                                                                                            |
| Fixed                | Privacy policy updated to match, in English and the nine translations: bookings kept as anonymous records once the account closes; notes and receipts kept until the account closes; reviews and questions removed on closing; Event Staff requests kept without contact details. "Last updated" is now 20 November 2026.                                                                                                                      |
| Missing from the PRD | The full list of what closing does: signs out every device, cancels upcoming bookings (the place is told), erases booking notes, deletes saved places, receipts, reviews and questions, erases Event Staff contact details, and anonymises the account.                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Open                 | Emails already sent stay in the email provider's logs until the provider deletes them; the privacy policy could say so.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Open                 | `ContentPage.test.tsx` fails on this branch with "Caveat is not a function" (a design font in the tests); unrelated to these changes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
