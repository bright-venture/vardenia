# Authentication PRD: audit notes

Notes from auditing "Vardenia Authentication and Accounts" (PRD dated 20 November 2026), section by section, against the code on `feature/business-onboarding-form` and live tests on the development database.

Each note is one of:

- **Missing from the PRD**: true in the product, but the PRD does not say it. Add it to the next PRD version.
- **Correction**: the PRD says something the product does not do.
- **Fixed**: a gap found and fixed during the audit (the PRD can keep its wording).
- **Open**: needs a decision or a later step.

## What is left to do, decide or check

Steps 1 to 10 grouped by what is left to do. Each item says what it is and why it matters; the step tables below keep the evidence. When this list is empty, the PRD and the product match. What was fixed during the audit is in the step tables below.

### 1. PRD wording to update (no code change)

These make the PRD describe what the product really does.

- **Sign-in limits** (section 2): two counters for sign-in, sign-up, forgot password, reset and verify: 10 a minute per internet connection and email, and 60 a minute per connection; requests with no email share 10 a minute per connection. "Forgot password" once per 15 seconds per account. The account lock (10 wrong for a customer, 5 for a partner or team member, 10 minutes, reset by a correct sign-in) is unchanged. The PRD only mentions the lock.
- **Profile changes** (section 2): changing the password or the email asks for the current password and signs out every other device; a password change is confirmed by email to the account; an email change must be confirmed from the new address (until then the account cannot be used on any device), and the old address is told, with the contact address. The PRD does not describe profile changes.
- **Public actions without an account** (section 3): sign-up and the business form (anti-bot check and limits); forgot password and resend verification (limits, always the same answer, one email per 15 seconds per account, no anti-bot check on purpose so a locked-out person is not slowed down); the Questions page form (anti-bot check, a hidden trap field, a limit counted across all servers; the team answers from the admin); links from emails (signed for one booking or account, single use, time-limited). The PRD only names the business form.
- **Event Staff** (section 3): requesting hostesses or waiters needs a signed-in guest with a confirmed email; the confirmation goes to the account's own address, the request appears on the account page, and the team answers from the admin; a visitor is asked to sign in or create an account first. Decided 21 November 2026: keep requiring sign-in. The PRD does not mention Event Staff.
- **Anti-bot check (Turnstile)** (section 3): sign-up, the business form and the Questions form are checked. If the keys are missing the check is skipped, and the admin home shows a Site setup warning ("Anti-bot check is off", or "Anti-bot check cannot work" when only the site key is missing, which would refuse every sign-up); if Cloudflare cannot be reached, people are let through and the outage is logged. Production has both keys (checked 21 November 2026, including that the build receives the site key).
- **Sign-up limit** (section 4), wording agreed 21 November 2026: "Sign-up is limited to 10 a minute per internet connection and email, and 60 a minute per connection, shared with sign-in, forgot password, reset and verify."
- **Booking limits** (section 4), changed 21 November 2026: "A customer can have at most 5 booking requests waiting for an answer, and at most 15 upcoming bookings in total (pending or confirmed). A place that confirms on the spot is not held up by the 5." The PRD says 5 pending.
- **Closing an account** (section 4), wording agreed 21 November 2026: "A customer can close their account from the account page (password required). It is always anonymised, never deleted: name, email, phone and password are replaced, every device is signed out, upcoming bookings are cancelled (the place is told), booking notes are erased, and saved places, receipts with their photos, reviews and questions are deleted; Event Staff requests keep the event but lose the contact details. Bookings stay for good as anonymous records."
- **Customer pages** (section 4): add the "close account" page; the reset link also verifies an unverified account.
- **"The team never sees a password"** (section 5): true only for partners created by publishing; a partner created by staff starts with the password staff typed (accepted as fine on 20 November 2026).
- **What partners edit** (section 5): today bookings, closed dates, rooms, open dates and hotel times, not the listing content; update when listing editing lands.
- **Which listing fields are hidden** (section 5): tier, verified and listing number are public by design; contract dates, commission and submission details are hidden.
- **"Booking fees" means commission** (section 6), plus the full list of admin-only deletes, now including articles, photos and areas.
- **Admin sets the tier** (section 7): the PRD says staff do it during review, but only an admin can.
- **New submission rules** (section 7): contract end date required to publish; Rejected is final; the four statuses shown as State; old place requests removed; a submission starts as tier Silver; photos JPEG, PNG or WebP only.
- **Scheduled jobs run only on the live site** (section 7): hiding expired contracts and expiring unanswered bookings never happen on staging, which matters when testing there.
- **Listing numbers** (section 8): after the release, enter the total and ranges on the live site (staging already has them); the total cannot drop below the last range end; ranges cannot be moved while used.
- **Open items list** (section 10): remove "race on a taken number" and "ranges can be moved" (both done); reword "staging database" (it is the live database that needs the ranges); "numbering-only issue" does not apply, All Lebanon is the real printed issue and stays public; write in the answer for "partner after contract ends" (still signs in and sees the listing, bookings refused, page hidden).
